## Precision Semi-Solid Rheocasting

Semi-solid rheocasting (SSR) fills the gap between conventional high-pressure die casting (HPDC) and forgings — delivering near-net-shape structural components for automotive, EV, and aerospace programs. Our 400T–3000T casting fleet and robotic pouring lines produce repeatable magnesium and aluminum components.

### Why Rheocast

- **Lower porosity tendency** — thixotropic slurry reduces gas entrapment versus HPDC (industry reference: HPDC typically 2–5%).
- **Tight tolerances** — CNC-ready surfaces; achievable CNC tolerance to ±0.01 mm, confirmed per project and geometry.
- **Lightweight edge** — ideal for EV battery housings and structural brackets.

> **Capability notes (project-dependent):** The values above — minimum wall thickness (typically 1.2 mm), CNC tolerance (±0.01 mm), and tonnage range (400T–3000T) — are typical achievable targets. The actually manufacturable values for any given part depend on flow distance, material, gating/feeding concept, mold temperature, part structure and required clamping force, and are confirmed during the DFM review. They are not blanket guarantees.
