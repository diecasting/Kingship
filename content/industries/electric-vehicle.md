## Capabilities for electric vehicles

- Battery pack structures, trays and enclosures in ADC12 and A356 aluminum
- Motor, inverter and on-board charger housings with tight sealing requirements
- Lightweight magnesium brackets and structural members, subject to project feasibility review
- Project-defined CNC post-machining, leak testing and X-ray/CMM inspection planning

Casting and CNC requirements are reviewed together, with process route, tolerance and inspection scope confirmed for each EV component.
