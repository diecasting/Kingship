## Precision Semi-Solid Rheocasting

Semi-solid rheocasting (SSR) fills the gap between conventional HPDC and forgings — delivering near-net-shape, low-porosity structural parts for automotive, EV, and aerospace programs. Our 400T–2500T fleet and robotic pouring lines produce repeatable, defect-free magnesium and aluminum components.

### Why Rheocast

- **Low porosity by design** — thixotropic slurry eliminates gas entrapment.
- **Tight tolerances** — ±0.01 mm CNC-ready surfaces straight from the die.
- **Lightweight edge** — ideal for EV battery housings and structural brackets.
