## Capabilities for automotive

- Powertrain and transmission housings in ADC12, A380 and A356 aluminum
- Lightweight structural and seat components in AZ91D and AM60B magnesium
- Low-porosity semi-solid rheocast parts for safety-critical brackets
- 3/4/5-axis CNC machining, airtightness and pressure leak testing

Every automotive part is produced under IATF 16949 discipline with real-time X-ray porosity detection and full traceability, so assemblies meet durability and safety targets from the first article to serial delivery.
