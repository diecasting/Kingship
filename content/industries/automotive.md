## Capabilities for automotive

- Powertrain and transmission housings in ADC12, A380 and A356 aluminum
- Lightweight structural and seat components in AZ91D and AM60B magnesium
- Project-defined casting and inspection planning for structural or safety-related brackets
- CNC post-machining, leak/pressure testing and X-ray/CMM scope defined per automotive project

Inspection, testing and traceability requirements are agreed for each automotive project and recorded against the drawing, critical characteristics and customer quality requirements.
