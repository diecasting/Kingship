## Certificate documents

This page presents copies of the management-system certificate documents issued to Kingship (Dongguan) Precision Manufacturing Co., Ltd. Each card reproduces the exact standard, certificate number, issuing body, scope and dates printed on the document. The documents are shown as certificate copies; an online check with the issuing bodies was not available at the time of publication.

<div class="kcert-note-block">The dates shown are those printed on the certificate. Issuing-body online verification was not available when this page was published, so no statement is made here about current certification status.</div>
