## Precision Die Casting Tooling

From DFM-driven die design to mold-flow-validated cavities, we deliver H13/SKD61 tooling that survives 100k–500k shots for aluminum and magnesium structural casting programs.

### Tooling readiness

- **EDM cavity machining** for crisp, reusable die details.
- **High-speed tool milling** for tight-tolerance mold surfaces.
- **Mold-flow simulation** de-risks fill, cooling, and warpage before first cut.

## Why In-House Tooling Matters

Owning the tool room lets us align DFM, simulation, and tryout with production casting instead of handing off between vendors. The result: shorter lead times, fewer interface errors, and a single accountable PPAP for the whole cast + tool program.

### Tool Design & DFM Methodology
We design dies in Pro/E and UG with draft, fillet, and gating review up front. DFM feedback flags thin sections, trapped features, and ejector conflicts before steel is cut.

### Tool Steel & Cavity Configuration
H13 hot-work and SKD61 die steel support 1–8 cavities with 100k–500k shot life. Cavity count is balanced against cycle time and annual volume to minimize piece cost.

### Mold Flow Simulation
Magma and Moldflow simulation validates fill, cooling, and warpage, de-risking first-article yield on every new program.

### Tool Maintenance & Lifecycle
Preventive maintenance schedules, spare inserts, and reconditioning keep tools at tolerance across their lifecycle. We track shot counts and surface condition per cavity.

### Lead Time & Cost Drivers
Tool cost is driven by cavity count, steel grade, and simulation scope. Bridge tooling shortens time-to-first-part for validation; hardened production tools amortize over volume.
