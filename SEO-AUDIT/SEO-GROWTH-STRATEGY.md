# KingShip SEO Growth Strategy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-22  
**Scope:** `https://kingship.alumcasting.com/`  
**Operating mode:** Evidence-based autonomous SEO execution inside the KingShip repository

## 1. Growth priorities

1. Increase qualified Google organic clicks.
2. Improve commercial visibility for CNC machining, aluminum die casting, magnesium die casting, tooling and manufacturing engineering.
3. Increase RFQs from engineers, sourcing teams, program managers and OEM manufacturers.
4. Strengthen technical credibility without expanding unverified manufacturing claims.

## 2. Brand and evidence boundary

- KingShip is treated as an independent site and brand.
- AlumCasting content, keywords, project history and capability claims are not automatically transferable.
- Shared legal-entity evidence is usable only within its documented scope.
- IATF 16949 evidence must retain the certificate holder and `die-cast parts and machined parts` scope; Clause 8.3 product design remains excluded.
- Equipment photographs prove that a process or equipment type was visible, not fleet size, ownership, calibration, capacity, product tolerance or inspection coverage.
- Missing evidence is recorded as HOLD rather than converted into a new number or promise.

## 5. 90-day execution plan

### Weeks 1-2: Intent and truth reset

- Fix the blog hub and CNC commercial intent.
- Remove unsupported absolute claims from high-priority commercial pages.
- Keep URL, Canonical, Redirect, Sitemap, public Schema template and RFQ service configuration unchanged.
- Establish a single baseline for queries, pages, countries and devices.

### Weeks 3-4: Commercial-page depth

- Improve aluminum and magnesium service pages using verified process boundaries.
- Add RFQ input sections to the main service pages.
- Strengthen blog-to-service internal links without creating duplicate URLs.
- Keep EN and all translated versions aligned on equipment, tolerance, material and inspection scope.

### Weeks 5-8: Engineering authority

- Expand existing guides on porosity control, X-ray/CMM planning, tooling and cast-to-CNC interfaces.
- Add source notes for externally verifiable technical claims.
- Improve translated metadata only after the English intent is validated.

### Weeks 9-12: Conversion and performance review

- Review 28-day and 56-day post-change GSC data.
- Compare commercial query coverage and page-level CTR.
- Identify whether organic visitors reach `/contact/`; no conversion rate is claimed without GA4 or RFQ source data.
- Reprioritize based on evidence, not content volume.

## 6. Measurement rules

- Use fixed, non-overlapping date windows.
- Do not add parent-domain and subdomain totals.
- Search Analytics returns top visible rows, not the complete query universe.
- Keep page and query changes separate from ordinary ranking volatility.
- Do not infer RFQ conversion rate without reliable event or CRM data.
