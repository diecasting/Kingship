## 3. 30 / 60 / 90-Day Plan

### Days 1-30

1. Remove unsupported 3000T/5000T, current-certification and every-part claims from home and industry pages across seven languages.
2. Obtain approval for Service Schema brand/description and Blog Alt patches.
3. Fix RFQ label localization after form-integration review.
4. Add an analytics instrumentation report and identify the source of `rfqSuccess`.
5. Refresh GSC baseline after any approved publication.

### Days 31-60

1. Improve Aluminum with neutral alloy-selection, DFM, inspection and RFQ modules.
2. Improve Tooling with tooling scope, validation and maintenance questions.
3. Expand ES/FR/JA Aluminum pages to match verified content depth.
4. Create at most two new English source articles from Clusters D/E; each must have primary-source notes and an approved image or diagram.
5. Localize only articles with evidence and measurable local-market relevance.

### Days 61-90

1. Review commercial-page CTR and ranking changes against the 28/56-day baseline.
2. Build localized versions of the best-performing safe article.
3. Improve internal links between technical articles and service/RFQ pages.
4. Review mobile RFQ usability with controlled test data.
5. Prune or redirect legacy taxonomy/user URLs only after a documented, approved mapping.

## 4. Success Measures

- Fewer unsupported P0 claims in every language.
- Increased commercial landing-page impressions without increased factual risk.
- CTR improvement on pages already positioned 4–20.
- Increase in verifiable form starts/submissions if analytics is connected.
- No increase in broken links, Canonical errors or hreflang mismatches.
- No new thin or duplicate URLs.

## 5. Guardrails

- No production deployment, commit, push or merge.
- No public Schema/template edits without approval.
- No external emails or customer contact.
- No price, capacity, PPM, tolerance or lead-time claims without evidence.
- No image reuse without authorization.
